How the tach estimate is made

Starts from the newest real tach reading — a shop entry, or the Tach End entered in ForeFlight — and adds ForeFlight flight time logged since. Read the tach before relying on it. Flights logged after the last data refresh (10-Nov-2026) are not included.

What isn't covered yet

Pre-2023 history comes from the OCR'd legacy records PDF and the shop entries attached to Savvy tickets; poor pre-2014 scans may hide items. ADs shown are those named in the records plus a targeted search, not a full AD search for the airframe, engine and prop.
